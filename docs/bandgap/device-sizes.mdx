# Bandgap Device Sizes

Every transistor in the [bandgap reference](./index.mdx) core, `xschem/bandgap_core.sch`, as xschem netlists it.
All are single devices (`nf=1`, `m=1`) unless noted. Sizes are in µm.

## MOSFETs

| Device | Role | Model | W (µm) | L (µm) | W/L |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| M1 | PTAT mirror, branch A | `sky130_fd_pr__pfet_01v8_lvt` | 8 | 4 | 2 |
| M2 | PTAT mirror, branch B | `sky130_fd_pr__pfet_01v8_lvt` | 8 | 4 | 2 |
| M3 | Output mirror | `sky130_fd_pr__pfet_01v8_lvt` | 8 | 4 | 2 |
| M4 | Amplifier tail | `sky130_fd_pr__nfet_01v8` | 8 | 4 | 2 |
| M5 | Amplifier input (BH, diode side) | `sky130_fd_pr__nfet_01v8_lvt` | 16 | 4 | 4 |
| M6 | Amplifier input (AH) | `sky130_fd_pr__nfet_01v8_lvt` | 16 | 4 | 4 |
| M7 | Amplifier load, diode | `sky130_fd_pr__pfet_01v8_lvt` | 4 | 4 | 1 |
| M8 | Amplifier load, output | `sky130_fd_pr__pfet_01v8_lvt` | 4 | 4 | 1 |
| M9 | Start-up, weak pull-up | `sky130_fd_pr__pfet_01v8` | 0.42 | 8 | 0.05 |
| M10 | Start-up, VREF sense | `sky130_fd_pr__nfet_01v8` | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| M11 | Start-up, pulls PB down | `sky130_fd_pr__nfet_01v8` | 0.42 | 1 | 0.42 |
| M12 | Tail-bias mirror | `sky130_fd_pr__pfet_01v8_lvt` | 8 | 4 | 2 |
| M13 | Tail-bias diode | `sky130_fd_pr__nfet_01v8` | 8 | 4 | 2 |

The matched devices all use L = 4 µm for matching and output resistance. M7 and M8 carry half the current of M1 to M3
at half the W/L, so all of them run at the same $V_{SG}$. Why each device ended up with this model and size is on [Circuit tuning](./tuning.mdx).
